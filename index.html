Tools, guardrails and data sources

Tools: policy lookup and claim history (synthetic) · geocoding (OpenStreetMap) · earthquakes (USGS) · rainfall and river flow (Open-Meteo) · news (Tavily) · date calculator. Plain-code guardrails override unsafe routes (e.g. no auto-approve without evidence). The agent can never reject a claim. Every step is logged. Demo only, not a production underwriting system.
